# SceneForge AI - Demo Video Script

## 🎬 Demo Video Outline (2-3 minutes)

### Opening (0:00-0:15)
- **Visual:** Logo animation with gradient background
- **Text on screen:** "SceneForge AI - ElevenLabs × turbopuffer Hackathon"
- **Voiceover:** "What if you could create a playable game scene just by describing it?"

### The Problem (0:15-0:30)
- **Visual:** Split screen showing complex game engines
- **Text:** "Game development is hard"
- **Voiceover:** "Creating game environments traditionally requires coding skills, 3D modeling expertise, and hours of work."

### The Solution (0:30-0:45)
- **Visual:** SceneForge AI interface
- **Action:** Type "A dark forest at night with a creepy cabin and wolf howls"
- **Voiceover:** "With SceneForge AI, just type a description and watch as AI generates a complete, playable game scene."

### Feature Showcase (0:45-2:00)

#### 1. AI Scene Parsing (0:45-1:00)
- **Visual:** JSON output showing structured scene data
- **Text:** "GPT-4 parses your description"
- **Voiceover:** "First, OpenAI GPT-4 analyzes your prompt and extracts scene elements, themes, and mood."

#### 2. turbopuffer Vector Search (1:00-1:15)
- **Visual:** Similar scenes appearing
- **Text:** "turbopuffer finds similar scenes"
- **Voiceover:** "Using turbopuffer's vector search, we find similar scenes to inform our audio and visual decisions."

#### 3. ElevenLabs Audio Generation (1:15-1:35)
- **Visual:** Audio waveforms generating
- **Text:** "ElevenLabs generates music, SFX, and voice"
- **Voiceover:** "ElevenLabs creates background music, positional sound effects, and even AI voice narration that describes your scene."

#### 4. Three.js Game Rendering (1:35-2:00)
- **Visual:** Game scene loading, player moving around
- **Action:** Show WASD movement, clicking objects, day/night toggle
- **Voiceover:** "Finally, Three.js renders a fully playable 3D scene you can explore right in your browser."

### Social Features (2:00-2:30)
- **Visual:** Gallery page, scene cards
- **Action:** Click "Remix" on a scene, show sharing options
- **Voiceover:** "Every scene is shareable with a unique link. Browse the gallery for inspiration, or remix existing scenes to create your own variations."

### Closing (2:30-3:00)
- **Visual:** Montage of different scenes
- **Text:** "Built with ❤️ for the ElevenLabs Hackathon"
- **Voiceover:** "SceneForge AI - where imagination becomes playable reality."
- **CTA:** "Try it now at sceneforge.onrender.com"
